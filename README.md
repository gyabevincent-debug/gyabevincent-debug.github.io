## What is AnBoWork?

AnBoWork is a **desktop AI Agent workbench that brings models, tools and your task environment into one app**, for Windows, macOS and Linux. It runs the official AI command-line tools installed on your computer, and you sign in to them with your own account. Sessions, history, MCP, Skills and long-term memory are managed in one place. Complex tasks start with a plan that you approve before anything runs, and the result is an editable Word, Excel, PowerPoint or PDF file, a website, or a code project.

This is the official AnBoWork blog. It explains what each feature is, who it suits and where its limits are. The articles are written in Chinese.

## How do I get started?

1. **Download and install.** Pick the installer for your system on the [download page](https://www.anbowork.com/download).

## FAQ

**Is AnBoWork free?**
The client is a free download. Platform models are billed by actual usage, and the dedicated network exit is an optional subscription. For prices see the [official website](https://www.anbowork.com/packages).

**Does AnBoWork read my account password?**
No. AnBoWork runs the official AI command-line tools installed on your computer, and you sign in inside the official program. The login stays on your computer, is not passed through AnBoWork, and AnBoWork does not read your account password.

**Does enabling the dedicated network exit change my computer's network settings?**
No. The connection settings only apply to AnBoWork's own AI requests. There is no system proxy change and no virtual network adapter, and other apps keep using your existing network.

**Can it guarantee that an AI platform will work, or that the platform will not act on my account?**
No. The network exit is meant to improve connection stability. It must not be used to evade sanctions, regional restrictions, identity verification, platform security controls or any third-party terms of service, and it does not guarantee that a platform will not act on your account. Please use it within the laws where you live and follow each platform's terms of service.
