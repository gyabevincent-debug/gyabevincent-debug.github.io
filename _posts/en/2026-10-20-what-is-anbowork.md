## What is AnBoWork?

AnBoWork is a **desktop AI Agent workbench** for Windows, macOS and Linux. A chat window waits for your questions; a workbench is built around handing tasks to an AI. You describe what you want in plain language, and the AI works on it inside the same app: conversation, writing, organizing material and everyday office work.

## What does it bring together?

- **Official AI tools, with your own account.** AnBoWork runs the official AI command-line tools on your computer, and you sign in to them yourself. See [native account sign-in]({% post_url en/2026-10-20-native-account-login %}).
- **Sessions, history, MCP, Skills and memory in one place.** Saved project background and preferences can carry across models and conversations, so you explain less each time.
- **A plan before the work.** For complex tasks the AI first clarifies the goal and reads your material in read-only mode, then submits a plan. You can annotate, adjust or reject it, and nothing is carried out until you approve.
- **Results you can keep editing.** The output can be a Word, Excel, PowerPoint or PDF file, a website or a code project.
- **An optional dedicated network exit.** Once enabled, AI requests keep a consistent network path. See [a stable network for your AI accounts]({% post_url en/2026-10-20-stable-ai-network %}).

## Who is it for?

- People who write or organize material often
- People who want to hand repetitive office routines to an AI
- People who want to use several large models from one app

## How do I get started?

1. Open the [download page](https://www.anbowork.com/download) on the official website.
2. Pick the installer for your system and install it.
3. Create an account, then describe your task in plain language.
