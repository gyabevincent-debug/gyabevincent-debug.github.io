> In short: when you use Claude, Codex or Antigravity in AnBoWork, you sign in with **your own account on the official service**, not an account somebody else relays to you.

## What is native account sign-in?

Native account sign-in means using the account that you registered and subscribed to yourself on the official service. The account is yours, the subscription is yours, and the sign-in happens in the official service's own login flow. AnBoWork runs the official command-line tools installed on your computer, so the login stays on your machine and AnBoWork does not read your account password.

For these services AnBoWork supports **only this one sign-in method**:

- **Claude**
- **Codex**
- **Antigravity**

## Why only native account sign-in?

The choice has a few direct benefits:

1. **Clear ownership.** The account is always yours. Nothing is borrowed or shared, and you control its permissions and subscription status.
2. **Usage and billing stay with the official service.** You keep checking your subscription, usage and bills in each official account, with no third party to reconcile against.
3. **A simple boundary.** AnBoWork does not offer relayed accounts from third parties, so how it works is easy to see.

## How do I start?

1. Download and install AnBoWork from the [official website](https://www.anbowork.com/download).
2. Open it and choose the service you want, such as Claude, Codex or Antigravity.
3. Sign in with your own official account.

## What should I know before using it?

- You need your own working account or subscription for each service. AnBoWork does not provide these accounts.
- Each service's terms of use are set by that service. Please follow them.
